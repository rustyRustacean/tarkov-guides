## Understanding Crosshair Placement: Pre-Aim and Discipline

One of the biggest reasons players lose fights isn’t reaction speed, it’s poor crosshair placement. This tutorial explains how to keep your aim ready before the fight starts so your shots land instantly instead of wasting milliseconds correcting your aim.

### The Core Rule: Always Keep Your Crosshair at Head Height

Every gunfight in Tarkov begins with where your crosshair is sitting. Keep it anchored at head level whether moving through halls, climbing stairs, or rounding corners. If your crosshair is aimed at the floor or center mass, you’ll waste precious time dragging your aim upward, giving your opponent the first lethal shot.

### Pre-Aiming: Predict Before You Peek

Winning duels in Tarkov often comes down to who’s ready first. Before exposing yourself, pre-aim where the enemy’s head is likely to appear. You don’t want to react to a target; you want your sights already waiting for them. This turns unpredictable encounters into near-instant headshots.

### Crosshair Discipline During Movement

Movement and crosshair control must stay linked. As you transition between cover or check corners, keep the crosshair level and centered on potential head locations, never drifting to the floor or ceiling. Remember the clip in the previous tutorial? In that example, my gun is aimed at the head height of the door. Throughout your raids, continually remind yourself to aim at head height. If you're used to aiming center mass, it should feel slightly uncomfortable with how high your aim will feel. Remember, your goal from this tutorial is to remove the need to consciously adjust mid-fight.

### Map Knowledge and Angle Familiarity

Another vital part of crosshair placement is map knowledge. As you gain experience, you'll start to learn where people feel comfortable sitting. Memorize these sightlines and adjust your aim height accordingly. This lets you pre-aim before they’re visible, giving yourself more time to kill before the desync's over.

### Analyzing Deaths and Correcting Crosshair Errors

If you often feel like enemies “tank” shots, review your deaths via a clipping software. More often than not, your crosshair was centered on their chest instead of the head. Every missed opportunity in Tarkov is a lesson in crosshair discipline. Aim higher, aim earlier, and make that your default behavior.

A simple correction drill can make this instinctive. Review your deaths frame by frame, note where your crosshair was before the first shot, and identify if it sat too low or off-center. Recreate that scenario offline with deliberate head-level aim until it becomes second nature.

### Key Takeaways

Keep your crosshair at head height at all times. Pre-aim enemy positions before exposing yourself. Maintain aim discipline during movement and never let your crosshair drift off the threat zone. Learn common engagement areas and record your deaths to identify bad habits. The goal is to make your first frame of aim the same as your final shot. Once this becomes habit, you’ll find most fights are won before they even start.
