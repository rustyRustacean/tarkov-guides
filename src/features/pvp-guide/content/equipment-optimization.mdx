## Weight Class Strategy and Optimization

Master the relationship between weight, movement effectiveness, and tactical capability.

### Understanding Weight Class Impact

**Movement mechanics by weight:**

**Ultra-Light (0-15kg):**

- Maximum movement speed and acceleration
- Minimal inertia effects for instant direction changes
- Fastest stamina regeneration rates
- Optimal for aggressive tactics and hit-and-run strategies

**Light (15-25kg):**

- Excellent movement characteristics with good protection
- Balanced approach for most tactical situations
- Good stamina efficiency for extended operations
- Ideal for versatile playstyles

**Medium (25-35kg):**

- Moderate movement capabilities with substantial protection
- Noticeable inertia effects requiring technique adaptation
- Standard raid weight for most players
- Requires movement technique adjustment

**Heavy (35-50kg):**

- Significantly reduced movement effectiveness
- Major inertia penalties affecting all techniques
- Slow stamina regeneration limiting mobility
- Requires specialized heavy tactics

**Ultra-Heavy (50kg+):**

- Severely compromised movement in all aspects
- Extreme inertia effects making advanced techniques difficult
- Very limited tactical mobility options
- Specialized applications only

## Advanced Keybind Configuration

Optimize your control scheme for maximum movement technique effectiveness.

### Movement-Centric Keybind Philosophy

**Core optimization principles:**

- Minimize hand movement between essential keys
- Separate critical functions to prevent input conflicts
- Maintain aim consistency during complex inputs
- Enable simultaneous multiple technique execution

**Essential keybind categories:**

- Primary movement keys (WASD + sprint/crouch)
- Lean and positioning controls (Q/E or alternatives)
- Weapon handling binds (ADS, fire modes, tactical devices)
- Tactical equipment access (medical, grenades, utilities)

**Hand position optimization:**

- Keep movement hand focused on WASD area
- Avoid grip changes during critical moments
- Maintain muscle memory consistency
- Enable rapid key combinations

### Advanced Lean Configuration

**Optimal lean binding strategies:**

**Traditional Q and E setup:**

- Most common and widely supported
- Natural finger positioning for most players
- Good for beginners learning lean mechanics
- Established muscle memory patterns

**Alternative C and V setup:**

- Reduces accidental lean activation
- Better separation from other functions
- Good for players with larger hands
- Prevents interference with weapon controls

**Mouse side button setup (advanced only):**

- Fastest lean execution for experts
- Requires high-quality gaming mouse
- Risk of grip changes affecting aim
- Not recommended for most players

**Lean integration considerations:**

- Avoid any setup requiring grip changes
- Maintain aim stability throughout lean execution
- Enable rapid lean switching for advanced techniques
- Prevent accidental lean activation during combat

### Equipment Access Optimization

**Tactical equipment binding hierarchy:**

**Primary tier (immediate access needed):**

- Medical items for emergency healing
- Primary grenades for tactical deployment
- Tactical device toggles for combat

**Secondary tier (frequent but not critical):**

- Secondary medical items
- Utility grenades (smoke, stuns)
- Weapon modification access

**Tertiary tier (situational access):**

- Rarely used medical items
- Specialized equipment
- Non-combat utilities

**Access speed optimization:**

- Most critical items get single-key binds
- Secondary items use easily accessible modifier combinations
- Rarely used items relegated to menu access or complex combinations
- Muscle memory development through consistent binding
